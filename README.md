# Fourier-Optics

This project explores **Fourier Optics** through mathematical analysis, numerical simulation, and experimental observation.

## Phase 1 — Theoretical Foundations

In the first phase, we studied the mathematical foundations of Fourier Optics, including the **2D Fourier Transform**, its properties, and the **Fresnel and Fraunhofer diffraction approximations**.

## Phase 2 — Diffraction and Optical Systems

In the second phase, we applied the theoretical results from Phase 1 to study diffraction from different optical apertures. For each case, we analyzed the diffraction pattern both **mathematically and through numerical simulations in Python**, and compared the results to see whether the simulations were consistent with the theoretical analysis and physical intuition.

Finally, we studied the behavior of **coherent and incoherent light** and examined how coherence affects the resulting optical patterns.
